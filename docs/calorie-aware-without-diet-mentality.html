I remember staring at food labels, feeling like I needed a degree in nutrition just to figure out what I was eating. It felt so overwhelming, and honestly, it made me dread mealtime. My goal was weight loss, but all I felt was restriction and deprivation. Sound familiar? I was convinced that to lose weight, I had to be on a perpetual diet, counting every single morsel. It was exhausting and, spoiler alert, it never worked long-term for me. I’d yo-yo back and forth, feeling guilty and defeated. But what if I told you there’s a way to be mindful of your calorie intake without the rigid rules and the constant feeling of being ‘on a diet’? It’s all about shifting your mindset from restriction to awareness. Think of it less like a diet and more like getting to know your body and its energy needs. It’s about making informed choices, not about punishing yourself for eating a cookie. This approach helped me finally break free from the diet cycle and build sustainable healthy habits.
