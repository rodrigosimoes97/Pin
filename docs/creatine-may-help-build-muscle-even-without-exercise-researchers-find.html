The Big Lie
Creatine only works if you are lifting heavy weights five days a week in a gym.
The Human Reality
Creatine increases cellular hydration and energy stores, supporting muscle preservation and growth even during periods of complete rest.

The Cellular Magic of Muscle Preservation

Creatine draws water directly into muscle cells, expanding cellular volume and sending a growth signal to tissues. This anabolic signaling pathway happens independently of mechanical tension from weightlifting.

For anyone recovering from surgery or suffering from chronic fatigue, this cellular swelling acts as a buffer against tissue breakdown. Read this another practical guide to learn how physical recovery impacts metabolism.

Researchers observed that immobilized limbs lost significantly less muscle mass when subjects supplemented with high-quality creatine monohydrate. It keeps satellite cells active, aiding in cellular repair while you rest.

Why Restful Recovery Multiplies Your Results

Your muscles don't just need cellular energy to stay full; they require deep cellular rest to repair without stress hormones getting in the way. High cortisol levels destroy muscle tissue faster than inactivity ever could.
