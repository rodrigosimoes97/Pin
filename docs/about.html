About

We publish evidence-informed, US-focused health content designed to be practical, scientifically grounded, and transparent.

Editorial Integrity & Scientific Principles

Our articles synthesize findings from peer-reviewed scientific journals and official health agencies, including the National Institutes of Health (NIH), Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), and Food and Drug Administration (FDA). We do not invent studies, clinical trials, or statistics, and we clearly distinguish preliminary findings from established medical consensus.

Educational & Non-Clinical Scope

All content on this site is published strictly for educational and informational purposes. It does not constitute medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional before making significant health, dietary, or medication changes.
