For years, researchers warned us about blood pressure spikes from sodium, but the real silent hit lands squarely on our cognitive wiring. When high-salt diets trigger gut-driven inflammatory signals, microvascular blood flow to our neurons takes a direct hit.

Recent studies zeroed in on short-chain fatty acids and specific dietary flavonoids that blunt this exact neurovascular breakdown. Essentially, scientists discover a natural compound that could protect the brain from too much salt by reinforcing the gut-brain barrier before systemic inflammation reaches delicate cortical vessels.
